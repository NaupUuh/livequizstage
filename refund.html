Refund Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · 14-day refund window on every license

14 days, no questions asked. If QuizStage is not right for your shows, email support@livequizstage.com within 14 calendar days of your purchase date. We refund the full $39 to your original payment method. You do not have to explain why.

1. What you are entitled to

Every QuizStage license comes with a 14-day refund window. If you request a refund inside that window, you receive 100% of the amount you paid. There is no restocking fee, no deduction for the time you used the Software, and no requirement to give a reason.

2. The exact process

Four steps, all handled by email. You do not need to fill in a form or open a ticket in a portal.

Step 1 — Email us within 14 days

Send a message to support@livequizstage.com from the email address you used at checkout, and put "Refund request" in the subject line. Day 1 is the calendar day of your purchase; the window closes at the end of day 14 in your local time zone.

Include these details so we can find your order on the first pass:

  • Your order number (from the purchase confirmation email), or
  • The full email address used at checkout, and
  • The approximate purchase date.

A one-line message such as "Order #1042 — please refund" is enough. A reason is welcome but optional.

Step 2 — We acknowledge within 2 business days

We reply to confirm that we have located the order and that it falls inside the 14-day window. If the request arrived after day 14, we tell you that in the same reply and explain what options remain under applicable consumer law.

Step 3 — The refund is issued

We submit the refund to the original payment method within 2 business days of acknowledging your request. The full purchase amount is refunded; we do not withhold transaction fees. You receive a confirmation email when the refund has been submitted.

Step 4 — Your license is deactivated

When a refund is processed, the license key associated with that order stops being valid. Uninstall the Software from your machine at that point. The refunded license cannot be reactivated; if you want to use QuizStage again later, you can buy a new license at the current price.

4. After the 14-day window

Requests received after day 14 are not eligible for a refund under this policy, because the license is a one-time purchase of delivered software rather than a subscription. If you believe your situation is exceptional — for example, the Software never activated on a supported machine and support could not resolve it — email us and describe what happened. We review those cases individually, and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Nothing in this policy reduces rights you may have under mandatory consumer-protection law in your country of residence.

5. Before you ask for a refund

Most refund requests we receive come from setup problems that take a few minutes to solve. If the show will not start, players cannot join, or the OBS overlay stays blank, email support first — we can usually fix it the same day:

  • Players cannot reach the join page — confirm the phones are on the same Wi-Fi network as the host laptop and that the router allows client-to-client traffic.
  • Overlay is blank in OBS — the Browser source URL should be http://127.0.0.1:8899/, and QuizStage must be running while OBS captures it.
  • Windows blocked the installer — allow the app through Windows Security and run the installer again.

None of this is required to get a refund. It is simply the faster path if you would rather keep the software and fix the problem.

6. Duplicate or accidental purchases

If you were charged twice for the same order, or bought a second license by mistake, contact us with both order numbers. Duplicate charges are refunded in full regardless of the 14-day window, because that is our error rather than a change of mind.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please email us before contacting your bank. A chargeback usually takes longer to resolve than a direct refund, and it suspends the license while the dispute is open. We would rather refund you directly and close the matter in days.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026

The short version: QuizStage is desktop software that runs entirely on your own Windows computer. It does not collect personal data about you or your players. This website uses no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no cookies. The only way we receive information is when you email us.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the QuizStage desktop application for Windows ("the Software") and to the website at livequizstage.com ("the Site"), both provided by QuizStage ("we," "us," "our"). It explains what information is and is not handled when you use the Software or visit the Site.

2. The Software runs locally

QuizStage is a local application. Everything the Software does during a quiz — hosting the show, serving the player join page, tracking answers, calculating scores, and rendering the OBS overlay — happens on your own machine and over your own local network.

  • No accounts. There is no sign-in, no user profile, and no QuizStage account for the host or for players.
  • No telemetry. The Software does not send usage statistics, crash reports, device identifiers, or diagnostic data to us or to any third party.
  • No player data leaves your network. Player display names, answers, lives, and scores exist only in the memory and local files of the host machine, for the duration of the show.
  • No internet requirement. A show runs with the network cable unplugged. The player join page is served from your laptop, not from a server we operate.
  • Question packs are yours. CSV files you import are read from your local disk. We never receive copies of them.

QuizStage does not include AI features, bots, simulated players, advertising modules, or social-media integrations. There is no mechanism in the Software that transmits your quiz content anywhere.

3. Players and their devices

Players join by opening a local address in a phone browser. That page is served by the host machine on the local network. We do not operate that server, we do not see its traffic, and we do not receive any information about players — not their names, not their device type, not their IP address, not their answers.

If you host a public quiz night, you are responsible for how you handle player names in your venue. QuizStage stores them only in local session data on your machine.

4. This website

The Site is a set of static pages. It does not use cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage, fingerprinting, or any analytics service. It does not embed advertising networks, social plug-ins, or third-party tracking pixels. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not sell or share visitor data, because we do not collect it.

Like any web host, the provider that serves these pages may keep standard server logs — such as the requested URL, a timestamp, and an IP address — for security and reliability purposes. Those logs are generated by the hosting provider, not by QuizStage, and we do not use them to identify or track visitors.

5. Purchases

When you buy a license, checkout is handled by a third-party payment processor. Your card details are entered on their systems and are never sent to or stored by us. We receive only what is needed to fulfill your order: the email address you used at checkout, the order identifier, the purchase date, and the license key issued to you. That information is used to deliver your license, provide support, and honor the 14-day refund policy described in our Refund Policy.

6. Email support

Email is the only support channel. If you write to support@livequizstage.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information solely to answer your request and keep a record of the support history. We do not add support addresses to a marketing list, and we do not sell or rent them.

Support messages are retained while your license is active and for a reasonable period afterward so that repeat issues can be resolved. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence at any time by emailing the same address.

7. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you explicitly ask us to.
  • We do not use cookies or tracking technology on the Site.
  • We do not collect data from children, and the Software is not directed at children under 13.
  • We do not transfer your data to advertisers or data brokers.

8. Legal disclosure

We may disclose information we hold if we are legally required to do so, for example in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or where necessary to protect our legal rights. Because we hold almost no data, such a request would in practice be limited to purchase records and support emails.

9. Your choices and rights

You can email support@livequizstage.com to request a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Deleting purchase records may limit our ability to reissue a license key or process a refund, so we will tell you what the effect would be before acting. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data-protection rights, we will honor applicable requests to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information.

10. Data retention

Purchase records are kept for as long as needed to support your license and to meet tax and accounting obligations. Support email is kept for the period described in section 6. Nothing else is retained, because nothing else is collected.

11. Security

License keys are delivered over email and validated by the Software. We recommend keeping your license key private. No system is perfectly secure, but the practical risk here is limited by design: there is no user database, no hosted player data, and no analytics store to breach.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes affecting how we handle support email or purchase records will be noted at the top of the page. Continuing to use the Software or the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in QuizStage generally, go to support@livequizstage.com. We answer email support requests in the order they arrive.
